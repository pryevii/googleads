# 9.2 Auction Insights Report

*Converted from `PPC Co Pilot/Ecom Accelerator/9. Optimization General/9.2 Auction Insights Report.rtf`*

Auction Insights report gives you data on competitors who you are competing with in the same ad auctions for your keywords.

You should be reviewing the Auction Insights report frequently to make sure you don’t see any changes in competitor aggressiveness or spot any new competitors entering the ad auctions.

1. Accessing the Report:

- Found under Insights and Reports > Auction Insights
- Can be viewed at account, campaign, or ad group level

2. Key Metrics:

- Impression Share: Indicates relative ad spend
- Overlap Rate: How often competitors appear in same auctions
- Position Above Rate: Frequency of competitors ranking higher
- Top of Page Rate: Frequency of ads appearing above organic results
- Absolute Top of Page Rate: Frequency of ads in position #1
- Outranking Share: How often your ads rank higher than competitors

3. Segmentation Options:

- Time (day, week, month, quarter, year)
- Device (desktop, mobile, tablet)

4. Practical Uses:

- Identify major competitors
- Gauge relative ad spend and aggressiveness
- Spot opportunities (e.g., weekends or specific devices)

5. Best Practices:

- Monitor regularly (e.g., monthly)
- Focus on position above rate for clearest competitive insights
- Use data to inform bidding and budget decisions

6. Limitations:

- Daily data may be limited; weekly or monthly views often more useful
- Metrics are relative, not absolute measures of performance

Remember, the Auction Insights report is a tool for understanding the competitive landscape and should be used in conjunction with other performance metrics for comprehensive account management.
