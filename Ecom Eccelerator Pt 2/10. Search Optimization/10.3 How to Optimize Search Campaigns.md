# 10.3 How to Optimize Search Campaigns

*Converted from `PPC Co Pilot/Ecom Accelerator/10. Search Optimization/10.3 How to Optimize Search Campaigns.rtf`*

**Core Optimization Philosophy**

The fundamental goal of optimization is simple: "More good, less bad." This means:

- Identifying what's working and scaling it
- Fixing or removing what isn't working
- Continuously looking for growth opportunities

**Campaign Structure Optimization**

**Consolidation Principles**

- Consolidate campaigns when possible, but segment strategically
- Modern best practices favor consolidation over excessive segmentation
- Old methods like single keyword ad groups (SKAGs) and Alpha/Beta are no longer recommended

**When to Segment Campaigns**

Always separate campaigns for:

- Brand vs. non-brand terms
- Different locations (especially international)
- Different product categories with varying price points
- Different business goals or margin requirements

**Campaign Settings Optimization**

**Critical Settings to Review**

1. **Conversion Goals**

- Verify correct conversion actions are being tracked
- Check for accidental double-tracking
- Ensure goals align with business objectives

2. **Network Settings**

- Confirm Display Network is off
- Review Search Partners performance and adjust accordingly

3. **Location Settings**

- Verify targeting is set to "Presence" not "Presence or interest"
- Check for any out-of-area traffic

4. **Budget Allocation**

- Identify campaigns limited by budget
- Reallocate budget from lower to higher performing campaigns
- Review budget distribution regularly

5. **Ad Schedule**

- Align with business hours for lead generation
- Review performance by time of day
- Adjust call extensions schedule to match business hours

**Ad Group Optimization**

**Structure Guidelines**

- Use Single Themed Ad Groups (STAG) instead of Single Keyword Ad Groups (SKAG)
- Group keywords by theme and intent
- Create separate ad groups when you can create more relevant ad copy or landing pages

**When to Create New Ad Groups**

- When you can create better targeted ads
- When keywords need more attention
- When performance varies significantly
- When landing pages differ

**Keyword Optimization**

**Adding Keywords**

- Look for search terms with at least 30 impressions
- Add both broad and exact match for top performers
- Don't add keywords just to add them
- Consider searcher intent and relevance

**Removing Keywords**

- Pause consistently poor performers
- Look for keywords with high clicks but low conversion rates
- Remove keywords significantly below goal metrics

**Match Type Strategy**

- Start with exact and phrase match
- Consider broad match only when:

- Campaign is well-optimized
- Using smart bidding
- Meeting performance goals

**Bidding Strategy**

**Optimization Sequence**

1. Fix campaign structure first
2. Optimize settings and keywords
3. Then focus on bidding
4. Remember: Bidding won't fix poor campaign structure

**Bidding Considerations**

- Use manual CPC or max clicks for new campaigns
- Switch to smart bidding after sufficient conversion data
- Set appropriate targets based on business goals
- Monitor and adjust regularly

**Regular Monitoring Tasks**

**Weekly Checks**

1. Review search terms report
2. Monitor competitor activity via auction insights
3. Check for disapproved ads
4. Review budget pacing
5. Monitor device performance

**Monthly Reviews**

1. Analyze campaign structure
2. Review audience performance
3. Check geographic performance
4. Evaluate bid strategy performance
5. Assess ad assets performance

**Google Recommendations**

- Review recommendations manually
- Don't enable auto-apply
- Evaluate each suggestion individually
- Consider business context before implementing

**Performance Metrics to Monitor**

- Conversion rate
- Cost per conversion
- Return on ad spend (ROAS)
- Search impression share
- Quality Score
- Click-through rate (CTR)

**Best Practices**

- Focus on structure before bidding
- Test meaningful changes
- Document optimizations
- Monitor competitor activity
- Regular review of search terms
- Keep ad groups themed and focused
- Use appropriate match types for campaign stage
