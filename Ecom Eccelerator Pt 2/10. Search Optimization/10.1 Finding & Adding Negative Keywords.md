# 10.1 Finding & Adding Negative Keywords

*Converted from `PPC Co Pilot/Ecom Accelerator/10. Search Optimization/10.1 Finding & Adding Negative Keywords.rtf`*

1. Definition and Importance:

- Negative keywords exclude specific search terms from triggering your ads
- Help focus budget on relevant searches and improve ROI

2. Types of Negative Keywords:

- Irrelevant searches
- Out-of-area locations
- Products you don't sell
- Competitor names (in non-competitor campaigns)

3. Finding Negative Keywords:

- Review search term reports
- Use Google Keyword Planner for research
- Utilize n-gram scripts or third-party tools
- Conduct regular negative keyword research

4. Adding Negative Keywords:

- Account level (impacts all campaigns)
- Campaign level
- Ad group level (use sparingly)
- Create negative keyword lists for efficient management

5. Match Types for Negative Keywords:

- Broad match: Excludes searches containing all words in any order
- Phrase match: Excludes searches containing the exact phrase
- Exact match: Excludes searches matching the keyword exactly

6. Best Practices:

- Use phrase match for most negative keywords
- Add both singular and plural forms
- Be cautious with short or ambiguous terms
- Regularly review and update negative keyword lists

7. Common Mistakes to Avoid:

- Adding overly broad terms that might exclude relevant traffic
- Forgetting to add variations (misspellings, singular/plural)
- Not reviewing negative keywords regularly

8. Negative Keyword Lists:

- Create themed lists (e.g., universal, brand terms, competitors)
- Apply lists to relevant campaigns for efficient management

9. Monitoring and Optimization:

- Regularly review search term reports for new negative keyword opportunities
- Check for any unintended exclusions

10. Advanced Techniques:

- Use exact match negatives to easily identify previously added terms in reports
- Consider using n-gram analysis for identifying problematic word combinations
