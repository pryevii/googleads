# 11.1 How to Optimize Shopping Campaigns

*Converted from `PPC Co Pilot/Ecom Accelerator/11. Shopping Optimization/11.1 How to Optimize Shopping Campaigns.rtf`*

**Product Feed Optimization**

The foundation of shopping campaign success starts with the product feed. Key areas to optimize:

1. Price optimization (most important factor)
2. Title optimization
3. Product images
4. Descriptions
5. Category mapping

**Campaign Structure Strategies**

**Core Principles**

- Maximize exposure for best-selling products
- Segment strategically based on business needs
- Consider account size and spend when choosing structure

**Common Segmentation Approaches**

1. Best Sellers vs. Other Products

- Separate high-performing products
- Ensure best sellers aren't limited by budget
- Give more control over bidding

2. Brand/Category Based

- Separate by product categories
- Split by brands when margins differ
- Group similar products together

3. Special Categories

- Sale products
- New products
- Seasonal items
- Zero-click products ("zombies")

**Query Sculpting Structure**

A three-tiered approach to control bidding based on search intent:

**1. Product-Specific Queries (PSQ)**

- Highest intent searches
- Example: "Nike Air Zoom Pegasus 38 size 10"
- Usually highest conversion rates
- Set to low priority
- Highest bids

**2. Brand Queries (BSQ)**

- Medium intent searches
- Example: "Nike running shoes"
- Set to medium priority
- Moderate bids

**3. General Queries (GSQ)**

- Lowest intent searches
- Example: "running shoes"
- Set to high priority
- Lowest bids

**Implementation Requirements**

- Must use shared budgets
- Proper priority level settings
- Strategic negative keyword lists
- Regular maintenance and monitoring

**Bidding Strategies**

**Starting Out**

1. Begin with Manual CPC or Max Clicks
2. Set conservative ROAS targets if using automated bidding
3. Focus on gathering conversion data

**Scaling Up**

1. Aim for 30-50 conversions before switching to smart bidding
2. Start with historical ROAS as target
3. Gradually adjust toward goal ROAS
4. Monitor and adjust based on performance

**Product Management**

**Excluding Poor Performers**

Before excluding products, analyze:

1. Feed quality issues
2. Price competitiveness
3. Seasonal factors
4. Historical performance
5. Sample size of data

**Revival Strategy**

1. Isolate poor performers in separate campaign
2. Test with minimal bids
3. Monitor for improvement
4. Return to main campaigns if performance improves

**Performance Monitoring**

**Key Metrics to Track**

1. Search Impression Share

- Above 50% typically good for shopping
- Below 50% indicates growth potential

2. Click Share

- Indicates market position
- Compare against benchmarks

3. Benchmark Metrics

- CPC benchmarks
- CTR benchmarks
- Price competitiveness

**Important Reports**

1. Merchant Center Reports

- Shopping experience scorecard
- Price competitiveness
- Visibility metrics
- Promotion performance

2. Auction Insights

- Competitor impression share
- Overlap rate
- Outranking share

**Performance Max Integration**

**Hybrid Approach**

1. Test moving best sellers to Performance Max
2. Keep other products in standard shopping
3. Structure based on account size:

- Simple: One Pmax + one standard campaign
- Advanced: Multiple Pmax + standard campaigns by category/brand

**Implementation Guidelines**

- Test with 70-80% of budget in Performance Max
- Maintain standard shopping for specific needs
- Regular performance comparison
- Adjust allocation based on results

**Best Practices**

1. Regular feed optimization
2. Consistent performance monitoring
3. Strategic budget allocation
4. Regular negative keyword updates
5. Competitive price monitoring
6. Test new campaign structures
7. Document performance changes
8. Monitor market trends
