# 3.1 Understanding Account Hierarchy

*Converted from `PPC Co Pilot/Ecom Accelerator/3. Account Setup & Structure/3.1 Understanding Account Hierarchy.rtf`*

**INTRO**

Before you build anything, you need to understand how Google Ads accounts are structured.

Think of it like a building:

- **Account** = The entire building
- **Campaigns** = Different floors
- **Ad Groups** = Rooms on each floor
- **Keywords/Products** = Furniture in each room

Simple concept. Let's break down each level.

**THE 4 LEVELS**

**Level 1: Account**

**What it is:**

- Your main Google Ads account
- Linked to one email address
- One billing method
- Represents your entire business

**Example:**

- Austin's Plumbing Company (Account)

**Level 2: Campaigns**

**What it is:**

- Major divisions within your account
- Where you set budget and locations
- Where you choose campaign type (Search, Shopping, Performance Max)

**What's controlled at campaign level:**

- Daily budget
- Geographic targeting
- Campaign type and settings
- Bid strategy

**Example:**

- Campaign 1: Search | Plumbing Services | Dallas
- Campaign 2: Search | Emergency Services | Dallas
- Campaign 3: Shopping | Plumbing Products

**Level 3: Ad Groups**

**What it is:**

- Subdivisions within campaigns
- Groups of related keywords or products
- Where you write your ads

**What's organized here:**

- Related keywords (for Search campaigns)
- OR related products (for Shopping campaigns)
- Your ads

**Example (inside "Search | Plumbing Services | Dallas"):**

- Ad Group 1: Water Heater Repair
- Ad Group 2: Drain Cleaning
- Ad Group 3: Pipe Repair

**Level 4: Keywords or Products**

**What it is:**

- The actual search terms you're bidding on (Search)
- OR the actual products you're selling (Shopping)
- The most granular level

**Example (inside "Water Heater Repair" ad group):**

- Keyword 1: water heater repair
- Keyword 2: water heater replacement

**WHAT EACH LEVEL CONTROLS**

**Account Level**

- Billing information
- Overall access and permissions
- Linked accounts (Google Analytics, Google Merchant Center)

**Campaign Level**

- **Budget** - How much you spend per day
- **Locations** - Where your ads show
- **Campaign type** - Search, Shopping, Display, Performance Max, etc.
- **Bid strategy** - How you bid for clicks/conversions
- **Networks** - Search Network, Search Partners, Display Network

**Ad Group Level**

- **Keywords or Products** - What triggers your ads
- **Ads** - What people see when they search
- **Bids** - Can be set at ad group level (or campaign level)

**Keyword/Product Level**

- **Individual performance** - Track each keyword/product
- **Match types** - How closely searches must match (Search only)
- **Bids** - Can be adjusted per keyword

**WHY THIS STRUCTURE MATTERS**

**Organization**

Different levels let you organize by:

- Service type (Campaign level)
- Product category (Campaign level)
- Specific themes (Ad Group level)
- Individual searches (Keyword level)

**Control**

Different levels give you control over:

- Budget allocation (Campaign)
- Geographic targeting (Campaign)
- Ad relevance (Ad Group)
- Bid adjustments (All levels)

**Reporting**

Different levels let you analyze:

- Overall account performance
- Which campaigns are profitable
- Which ad groups convert best
- Which keywords drive results
