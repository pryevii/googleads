Building Your First Google Ads Campaign for E-commerce

1. Assess Readiness:

- Ensure sufficient budget (aim for at least 10 clicks per day)
- Verify your website's conversion capability
- Ensure competitive pricing
- Commit to a long-term investment (at least 3 months)

2. Focus on Shopping Ads:

- Allocate 80% of budget to shopping campaigns
- Create two main campaigns: a. Top Sellers Campaign b. Everything Else Campaign

3. Top Sellers Campaign:

- Feature best-selling products, brands, or categories
- Allocate most of the budget here
- Use manual CPC or Max Clicks with bid cap
- Aim for 30+ conversions per month

4. Everything Else Campaign:

- Include all other products
- Use very low bids
- Exclude top sellers
- Gather data cheaply on other products

5. Bidding Strategy:

- Use Keyword Planner for initial bid estimates
- For shopping ads, start with about half the suggested search ad CPC
- Monitor Benchmark Max CPC to adjust bids

6. Scaling and Optimization:

- Once reaching 30+ conversions/month, consider: a. Testing Target ROAS bidding b. Moving to Performance Max campaigns
- Gradually increase investment in Everything Else campaign
- Use converting search terms to build search campaigns
- Consider adding Dynamic Search Ads and remarketing campaigns

7. Continuous Improvement:

- Regularly analyze and optimize campaigns
- Expand to other campaign types as you gather more data
- Stay committed to long-term growth and optimization
