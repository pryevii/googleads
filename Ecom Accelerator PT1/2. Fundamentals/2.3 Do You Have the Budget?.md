# 2.3 Do You Have the Budget?

*Converted from `PPC Co Pilot/Ecom Accelerator/2. Fundamentals/2.3 Do You Have the Budget?.rtf`*

You should aim for 10+ clicks per day so you have enough data to optimize and also give the machine learning breathing room.

Average CPCs will vary by industry but you can look this up before you start by using Google Ads keyword planner tool.

If you are in an industry with higher CPCs ($20+) and you only have a budget of $500 per month - you will waste your money.

Do your research before starting to invest otherwise you will be left with very little data, very little leads or sales, and you will give up after a few months.

I’ve seen it too many times.

**Setting Expectations:**

- New campaigns, even for existing advertisers, can take up to 90 days to dial in
- Profitability may not be achieved in the first 90 days (longer in some cases)
- The goal of a new campaign is to have enough data by the end of 90 days to confidently optimize

Budgeting and Data Collection

- Understanding the significance of click volume

- Importance of gathering enough data for optimization
- Role of click volume in machine learning algorithms
- Minimum recommended click volume (10+ clicks per day)

- Researching average cost-per-click (CPC) in your industry

- Using Google Ads Keyword Planner to estimate CPCs
- Identifying industry benchmarks and competitor data
- Adjusting expectations based on industry norms

- Aligning budget with business goals and industry standards

- Ensuring sufficient budget to achieve desired click volume
- Avoiding underfunded campaigns that limit optimization potential
- Case studies of businesses with misaligned budgets and click volumes
