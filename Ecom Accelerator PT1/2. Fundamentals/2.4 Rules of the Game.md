# 2.4 Rules of the Game

*Converted from `PPC Co Pilot/Ecom Accelerator/2. Fundamentals/2.4 Rules of the Game.rtf`*

1. Don’t trust Google
2. Patience wins

1. All the long term advertisers winning at the game of Google Ads started where you are now but gutted it out to make it work over time. This is not get rich quick. If you can’t gut 3-6 months of potentially not making profit from ads then don’t start.

3. Have a goal and know your numbers

1. LTV, conversion rate from leads, margins etc…

4. If your offer is 💩 so will your results

1. Winners have the following dialed in:

1. Great offer
2. Great price
3. Great website
4. Great funnel (form fill to follow up)

5. Google Ads is tuning into a game of who can feed the machine learning the best data

1. The better data your give Google, the better quality you will get back
2. The more data you give Google, the easier the game becomes

6. There is no auto-pilot

1. Automation has come a long way over the years but that doesn’t mean you can set up your campaigns and then just stop looking after them.
2. Competitors will change. Google will change. Things will break.
3. You must keep an eye out on the data, asses the data, and make your next moves from there.
