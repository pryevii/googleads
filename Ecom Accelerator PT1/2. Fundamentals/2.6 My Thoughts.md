# 2.6 My Thoughts

*Converted from `PPC Co Pilot/Ecom Accelerator/2. Fundamentals/2.6 My Thoughts.rtf`*

Google Ads is the greatest advertising channel that has ever been created (IMO).

However, Google is out to only make shareholders more money which means every move Google makes is guaranteed to make them more ad revenue.

They own the entire ecosystem - from the search, to the competition, to the pricing - they own all of it and they give you some data.

With that said, Google Ads can turn into a complete money machine that no other advertising channel can provide…Money out → Money in that is measured.

I’ve had the privilege over the last 10+ years to witness many success stories. Companies who didn’t trust Google Ads that are now making millions per year as a direct result from their Google Ads campaigns.

If you are a business with a great offer and competitive pricing you can win at the game of Google Ads if the numbers are in your favor.
