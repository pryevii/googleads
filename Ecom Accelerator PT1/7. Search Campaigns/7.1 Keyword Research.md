# 7.1 Keyword Research

*Converted from `PPC Co Pilot/Ecom Accelerator/7. Search Campaigns/7.1 Keyword Research.rtf`*

1. Fundamentals:

- Define your goal and budget before starting
- Focus on high-intent terms
- Start with seed terms and expand from there

2. Keyword Intent Types:

- Informational: Users seeking information, not ready to buy
- Navigational: Searches for specific pages (e.g., logins)
- Commercial: Middle-of-funnel, category-level terms
- Transactional: Bottom-of-funnel, ready-to-buy terms

3. Keyword Research Tools:

- Google Search (including related searches)
- Google Ads account data
- Google Search Console
- Google Keyword Planner
- AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Google Gemini)
- Competitor research tools (e.g., SpyFu)

4. Keyword Planner Process:

- Start with a seed term
- Filter for relevant monthly search volumes (e.g., >30)
- Remove irrelevant brand terms
- Group keywords by theme or intent
- Consider negative keywords during research

5. Analyzing Keyword Data:

- Review search volume trends
- Consider cost-per-click estimates
- Use the forecast tool for potential reach and budget
