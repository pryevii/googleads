# 7.5 Creating Ad Assets

*Converted from `PPC Co Pilot/Ecom Accelerator/7. Search Campaigns/7.5 Creating Ad Assets.rtf`*

- Introduction to Ad Assets:

- Formerly known as ad extensions
- Dynamic snippets of information added to ads
- Increase ad visibility and click-through rates

- Types of Ad Assets:
  a) Sitelink Extensions: Links to specific pages on your website
  b) Callout Extensions: Short phrases highlighting key features or offers
  c) Structured Snippet Extensions: Lists of products, services, or categories
  d) Call Extensions: Phone numbers for direct contact
  e) Location Extensions: Business address and map information
  f) Image Extensions: Visual elements added to text ads
  g) Price Extensions: Showcase products or services with prices
  h) Promotion Extensions: Highlight special offers or discounts
  i) App Extensions: Direct links to mobile app downloads
  j) Business Name and Logo: Brand identification elements
- Best Practices:

- Create account-level assets as a baseline
- Develop specific assets for campaigns and ad groups
- Use all relevant asset types for maximum impact
- Regularly update and monitor asset performance

- Asset Creation and Management:

- Access assets through the Ads & Assets menu
- Create assets at account, campaign, or ad group level
- Provide detailed information for each asset type
- Use high-quality images and accurate information

- Automated Ad Assets:

- Google can automatically create assets
- Access settings through advanced options
- Consider turning off automated assets for more control

- Asset Display:

- Google controls which assets show and when
- Higher ad positions increase chances of asset display
- No guarantee all assets will show for every ad

- Tips for Success:

- Create at least 4 sitelinks with descriptions
- Use callouts to highlight unique selling points
- Regularly update price and promotion extensions
- Ensure location information is accurate and up-to-date

- Monitoring and Optimization:

- Regularly review asset performance
- Test different asset combinations
- Update assets to reflect current offers and information
