# 7.3 Writing Irresistible Ads

- Importance of Quality Ads:

- Better ads lead to lower traffic costs and more profitable campaigns
- Good ads improve click-through rates (CTR) and quality scores
- Higher quality scores result in lower cost per click (CPC)

- Objectives of Search Ads:

- Capture attention of searchers
- Encourage clicks
- Prime users for conversion
- Match the searcher's intent

e) Responsive Search Ads (RSA) Strategy: - Ensure ad copy aligns with search queries - Include relevant keywords in headlines - Differentiate from competitors - Highlight what makes your offer special - Provide a specific, low-commitment first step - Make the offer clear and enticing (e.g., free consultation) - Limit to 2-3 ads per ad group - Continuously test and optimize - Pause underperforming ads - Test themes rather than specific headlines - Review performance of different variations - Optimize based on theme performance

- Additional Tips:

- Consider using AI tools like ChatGPT for ad copy ideas
- Regularly review and update ad copy
- Ensure ads lead to relevant landing pages

- Common Mistakes to Avoid:

- Running too many ads without proper testing
- Neglecting to pause underperforming ads
- Ignoring the importance of a strong USP and CTA

- Measuring Success:

- Monitor click-through rates
- Track conversion rates and cost per conversion
- Analyze quality scores
