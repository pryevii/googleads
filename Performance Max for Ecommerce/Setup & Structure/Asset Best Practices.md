**Ad Copy Text**

- Biggest ad copy needle movers

- CTAs
- Offers (Price & Promotions)
- Benefits and Features

💡 **Pro Tip:** You don’t need to write 15 headlines. If you are just filling in fluff or variations of the same headline you already wrote then just stop

**Images**

- Avoid

- Adding text or logos over images (these can look bad in final form)
- Blurry images
- Generic images unrelated your Asset Group products
- Avoid buttons

- **YouTube Video**

🛑 **Warning:** If you don’t give Google a video they will create an Auto-generated video that looks like complete garbage

- Tips

- Customer testimonial videos work really well since Google will remarket your audiences
- Product demos
- Educational & entertaining
- User or influencer generated content
