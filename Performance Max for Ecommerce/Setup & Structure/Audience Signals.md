**Audience Signals**

- There are no keywords - you use Audience Signals to help steer Google’s machine learning in the right direction and then it will go off on its own over time.

- **Google will not just target your Audience Signal - it is simply a ping to the algorithm on you telling it what audiences are important to your products.**

- Audience Signals can Include:

- Custom Interests
- Remarketing Audiences
- Interest & Detailed Demographics - In-market & affinity

**Audience Signal Best Practices (AKA Keep it Simple)**

- **For new accounts:** Start with remarketinga audiences (website visitors & purchasers…), custom interest audiences, and In-market.
- **No Audience Signal:** If I have PMax campaign that has been running for a long time and is performing well then I don’t even use Audience Signals anymore when building out new Asset Groups. Google knows this campaign already and will just bypass the Audience Signal anyways.

🛑 **Warning:** Don’t build out duplicate Asset Groups to test different Audience Signals. Google will just split your traffic between them. I’ve seen accounts get wrecked doing this.
