# Asset Optimization Setting

Google's Asset Optimization settings allow Google AI to enhance or generate assets using the information you provide. Understanding these settings is crucial for maintaining control over your Performance Max campaigns while leveraging automation effectively.

**Overview of Asset Optimization Settings**

Asset Optimization is found in the "Automatically created assets" section of your Performance Max campaign settings. This feature gives Google permission to create and modify your ad assets automatically. There are three main categories of asset optimization:

1. **Text Assets**
2. **Image Assets**
3. **Video Assets**

**Text Assets**

Text asset optimization includes two distinct options that control how Google can modify your ad copy:

**Text Customization**

This setting allows Google to create asset text from your site landing pages, existing ads, and provided assets. When enabled, Google can customize your ad copy automatically based on what it finds on your website.

1. Google pulls content from your landing pages
2. Creates variations of your existing ad copy
3. Customizes assets based on relevance signals

**Final URL Expansion**

This feature allows your Performance Max campaign to act more like a Dynamic Search Ad (DSA) campaign. When enabled, Google can send traffic to landing pages beyond what you specify in your final URL, based on what it determines will perform best for specific searches.

💡***Pro Tip:*** *If you enable Final URL expansion, make sure to exclude URLs you don't want Google sending traffic to. This could include blog posts, articles, or product categories not relevant to your specific campaign.*

**Image Assets**

Image asset optimization provides two options for automatically managing your visual creative:

**Image Enhancement**

This allows Google to adjust your uploaded images for better appearance across different ad placements. Google may crop, resize, or optimize images to fit various formats and improve visual appeal.

**Landing Page Images**

When enabled, Google can pull images directly from your landing pages to use in your ads. This expands your creative options without requiring manual uploads.

1. Google automatically identifies relevant images on your pages
2. Images are selected based on quality and relevance
3. Can expand your creative library without manual effort

**Video Assets**

**Video Enhancement**

This option allows Google to enhance your uploaded YouTube videos by creating additional versions optimized for different placements:

1. **Vertical versions** - Optimized for mobile and YouTube Shorts
2. **Square versions** - Better suited for certain display placements
3. **Shorter versions** - Condensed edits for quick-view placements

This functions similarly to responsive search ads but for video content—Google takes your uploaded video and dynamically creates variations to run across different video placements, including YouTube Shorts.

**Recommendations by Campaign Type**

Your asset optimization settings should align with your campaign structure and goals:

**Feed-Only Asset Groups (Starting Out)**

When running a feed-only Performance Max campaign (no creative assets, just product feed), you should turn off most asset optimization settings:

1. **Text Customization:** OFF
2. **Final URL Expansion:** OFF
3. **Image Enhancement:** OFF
4. **Landing Page Images:** OFF
5. **Video Enhancement:** OFF

**Full Asset Groups**

When running full asset groups with complete creative (images, videos, ad copy), you can be more selective with optimization settings:

1. **Text Customization:** OFF (or test with caution)
2. **Final URL Expansion:** ON (with URL exclusions)
3. **Image Enhancement:** ON
4. **Landing Page Images:** Test (be cautious)
5. **Video Enhancement:** ON

**Best Practices**

**Monitor Asset Performance**

If you enable text customization, regularly review the asset performance reports to ensure Google's automatically created copy aligns with your brand voice and messaging. Look for any assets that may be underperforming or off-brand.

**Use URL Exclusions with Final URL Expansion**

When enabling Final URL expansion, always set up URL exclusions to prevent Google from sending traffic to pages you don't want used in your campaigns:

- Blog posts and articles (unless relevant to your campaign)
- Product categories outside your campaign focus
- Contact, about, or policy pages
- Out-of-stock or discontinued product pages

**Start Conservative, Then Expand**

When first launching a Performance Max campaign, start with most asset optimization settings turned off. As your campaign matures and you have conversion data, you can test enabling additional features one at a time to measure their impact.

**⚠️ Warning: If you enable text customization without monitoring, Google may create ad copy that doesn't match your brand standards or contains messaging you wouldn't approve. Always review automated assets regularly.**

**Key Takeaways**

- Asset optimization settings control how much creative freedom you give Google's AI
- Feed-only campaigns should have all asset optimization turned off
- Full asset campaigns can benefit from Final URL expansion, image enhancement, and video enhancement
- Text customization requires careful monitoring if enabled
- Always use URL exclusions when enabling Final URL expansion
- Video enhancement allows Google to create multiple formats for different placements
- Start conservative and test features incrementally as your campaign matures
