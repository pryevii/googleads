# Insights & Search Term Report

*Converted from `PPC Co Pilot/Performance Max for Ecommerce/Optimization/Insights & Search Term Report.rtf`*

The insights & search term reports are where the money 💰 is.

**Search Categories vs. Specific Terms**

Google groups search terms into broader categories, but you can drill down to see the actual queries people searched. Pay attention to both levels—categories help you understand themes while specific terms reveal exact user intent.

**Audience Insights**

The report reveals which audience segments are driving performance, including demographic information and interest categories. This helps you understand if Google's AI is finding the right customers.

**Asset Performance**

While covered separately in asset optimization, the Insights report provides context on how different creative combinations perform across various placements.

**Actionable Optimizations from These Reports**

**Building Standard Search Campaigns**

When you identify high-converting search terms in the Search Terms report, extract these to build dedicated Standard Search campaigns. This gives you more control over bidding and ad copy for proven keywords while letting Performance Max continue discovering new opportunities. This is one of the most powerful ways to expand your account structure based on actual performance data.

**Adding Negative Keywords**

When you spot irrelevant or poor-performing search terms, add them as negative keywords. This prevents wasted spend on queries that don't convert.

**Identifying Search Theme Opportunities**

If you see search term gaps where Performance Max isn't capturing relevant queries, add these as Search Themes to your asset groups. The reports essentially show you where to direct Google's AI when it's missing important business themes.

**Reviewing Top Performing Audiences**

Use audience insights to inform targeting decisions across your entire account. If certain interest categories or demographics consistently convert well in Performance Max, consider building dedicated campaigns or adjusting audience signals accordingly.

**Monitoring for Brand Term Leakage**

Check if Performance Max is capturing brand searches, which can inflate apparent performance while not actually acquiring new customers. If you see significant brand traffic flowing through Performance Max, ensure your brand exclusions are properly configured and that you have a dedicated Brand Search campaign capturing that intent.
