**Negative Keywords**

**How to Add Negative Keywords**

**Option 1: Add directly at campaign level**

1. Go to your Performance Max campaign
2. Navigate to the negative keywords section
3. Add terms you don't want your ads to show for

**Option 2: Create and use lists** You can create negative keyword lists to apply across multiple campaigns.

**Creating a Negative Keyword List**

1. Click to create a new list
2. Add your keywords (example: "used", "free")
3. Hit "Save to new or existing list"
4. Name your list
5. Hit save

**Pro Tip: Create a Universal List**

Build a universal negative keyword list with terms that should NEVER trigger ads in any campaign across your account. Examples:

- "used" (if you don't sell used products)
- "free" (if you don't offer anything for free)
- Any other terms that are universally irrelevant to your business

**Where to Find Negative Keywords**

**Search Term Insights**:

- Go to the Insights tab in your Performance Max campaign
- Look at search categories
- Spot terms you don't want to show for
- Add them as negative keywords

**From Existing Search Campaigns**: If you're already running Search campaigns with negative keywords, apply those same negative keyword lists to your Performance Max campaigns. Don't reinvent the wheel - use the work you've already done.

**Negative Placements**

Placements are where your ads show up:

- Mobile apps
- YouTube channels
- Display network websites

**The Problem**: A lot of these placements are junk (dating apps, mobile games, low-quality sites) and waste your budget.

**Why the Interface Doesn't Work**

If you go to your Performance Max campaign → Content section → Exclusions and try to edit, you can't actually do anything. Google makes this harder than it should be.

**How to Find Your Current Placements**

1. Go to **Insights and Reports**
2. Click **Report Editor**
3. Scroll down to Template Gallery
4. Find "**Performance Max Campaign Placement**"
5. Pull 30 days of data (or your preferred timeframe)

**Limitation**: Google doesn't give you much data here - you can't even see clicks or cost, just impressions. But you can spot problematic placements like:

- Tinder (dating app)
- Metro Zone (game)
- Other mobile apps and games

**How to Actually Exclude Placements**

Since you can't exclude placements at the campaign level, you have to do it at the **account level**.

**Steps:**

1. Go to **Tools**
2. Click **Content Suitability**
3. Scroll down to **Excluded Placements**
4. This creates an account-level exclusion list that applies to ALL campaigns

**The Easy Way: Use Nils' Mobile App Exclusion List**

**Quick Win**: Exclude all mobile apps immediately to save budget.

A guy named Nils created a comprehensive list of mobile app categories that you can use. This list is available in the Skool community (shout out to Nils for sharing this).

<https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1bRYe0QyrrUa8qRxRFZ773yW050Gap2b-F74ip20Qh24/edit?usp=sharing>

**To use the list:**

1. Go to Content Suitability → Excluded Placements
2. Click to add exclusions
3. Paste Nils' entire mobile app category list
4. Hit Save

**Result**: You'll see something like "145 mobile apps excluded" in your campaign's Content section.

**Manual Method (If You Don't Use the List)**

1. Go to Content Suitability → Excluded Placements
2. Find "App Category"
3. Click through and manually exclude categories one by one
4. (This is pretty boring and time-consuming, so use the list if possible)

**Excluding Other Placements**

You can also exclude:

- **YouTube channels** you don't want to show on
- **Websites** you don't want to show on

Just add them in the Content Suitability section the same way you excluded mobile apps.
