# Our Performance Max Strategy: The Feeder Framework

*Converted from `PPC Co Pilot/Performance Max for Ecommerce/Fundamentals/Our Performance Max Strategy: The Feeder Framework  .rtfd`*

**Overview**

The landscape of Performance Max has fundamentally changed. While PMax remains a powerful tool in your Google Ads arsenal, our testing with 100+ clients throughout 2025 has revealed a critical insight: **Performance Max campaigns naturally gravitate toward warm audience traffic and existing customers rather than true new customer acquisition.**

This discovery has led us to completely restructure how we approach PMax campaigns. Instead of treating PMax as your primary acquisition engine, we now position it as a sophisticated remarketing and middle-of-funnel campaign that works in tandem with dedicated cold traffic acquisition campaigns.

Let's walk through what actually happens:

**Day 1:** User sees a Meta ad (or Google Shopping/Search ad) for a spill-proof tumbler

- They're interested but not ready to buy
- They click, browse for 2 minutes, then leave
- **This is your true first-click acquisition point**

**Day 3:** They start searching for tumblers

- They remember the product
- Performance Max remarketing kicks in
- Still not ready to purchase

**Day 4:** Ready to buy

- Performance Max captures the conversion
- Gets credit for the sale
- But did PMax *acquire* this customer? No.

**The Plateau Problem**

Most companies hit a wall with Performance Max at a certain spend level:

- You double your ad spend
- New customer acquisition barely moves (325 → 365 customers/week)
- nCAC (incremental new customer acquisition cost) nearly doubles ($27 → $47)
- **Why?** Because PMax is just spending more money on the same warm traffic pool

**The Feeder Strategy Framework**

**Core Concept**

Rather than fighting PMax's natural tendencies, we leverage them. Here's the new structure:

![79ffd5b1bd7b40d88ca2f0fa2a463716d1c5fc19c6ff4fd5a1fba07a311a27d7.png](Our%20Performance%20Max%20Strategy%3A%20The%20Feeder%20Framework%20%20.assets/79ffd5b1bd7b40d88ca2f0fa2a463716d1c5fc19c6ff4fd5a1fba07a311a27d7.png)

**Campaign Architecture**

**1. Feeder Campaigns (Primary Cold Traffic Engines)**

These are your *true* new customer acquisition campaigns:

**Standard Shopping (Non-Brand)**

- Aggressive bidding
- Focus on high-intent product searches
- Captures cold traffic at the moment of discovery
- Brand terms EXCLUDED

**Non-Brand Search**

- Exact, Phrase, and Broad match keywords
- Product category terms
- Problem-solution queries
- Brand terms EXCLUDED

**Optional Top-of-Funnel:**

- YouTube Demand Gen campaigns
- Meta/TikTok ads

**2. Performance Max (Middle/Bottom Funnel Remarketing)**

PMax's new role in this framework:

**Setup Differences:**

- Run at a **higher tROAS target** than feeder campaigns
- Acts as a "back channel" remarketing system
- Feeds off the audience data from feeder campaigns
- Let it "rabbit hole" on the highest-intent traffic you've already sent

**Configuration:**

- Brand terms EXCLUDED
- Higher ROAS goals force it to focus on warm, high-intent traffic
- Separate Brand Protection campaigns handle branded traffic

**3. Brand Protection Campaigns**

**Brand Search Campaign:**

- Exact match brand keywords
- Protects against competitors

**Brand Shopping Campaign:**

- Low priority setting
- Captures branded product searches
- Keeps brand traffic separate from non-brand and PMax

**Budget Allocation Strategy**

**Starting Framework**

For a business previously spending $10,000/month on PMax:

**New Structure:**

- Standard Shopping (Non-Brand): $4,000-5,000
- Non-Brand Search: $3,000-4,000
- Performance Max: $2,000-3,000
- Brand Protection: $500-1,000

**Scaling Framework**

**When to Scale Feeders:**

- nCAC is at or below your target
- New customer volume is increasing week-over-week
- Backend sales confirm the attribution
- Scale in 10-20% increments weekly

**When to Scale PMax:**

- Only after feeder campaigns are stable and scaling
- PMax should receive 10-20% increases
- Watch for diminishing returns (nCAC creep)

**Common Pitfalls to Avoid**

**❌ Don't Do This**

**1. Running Only PMax**

- You'll plateau quickly
- Heavily weighted toward repeat customers
- Limited control over new customer acquisition

**2. Letting Campaigns Overlap**

- PMax should NOT target your brand terms

**3. Making Drastic Changes**

- Don't cut PMax budget in half overnight
- Avoid massive tROAS swings
- Smart Bidding needs time to learn

**4. Ignoring Backend Data**

- Google Ads metrics lie about new customers
- Always verify with third-party attribution and backend data
- Track actual new customer revenue separately

**5. Over-Segmenting Too Early**

- Start consolidated
- Only segment when you have 50+ conversions/month per campaign
- More campaigns = more budget needed for learning

**✅ Do This Instead**

**1. Start Simple, Scale Complexity**

- Begin with one well-structured feeder campaign
- Add complexity as budget and data allow
- Always maintain the core framework

**2. Follow the Data**

- Let attribution guide your budget allocation
- Scale what actually acquires new customers
- Be willing to reduce PMax if it's not efficient

**3. Maintain Clear Documentation**

- Track your changes in a spreadsheet
- Note date, change made, and reason
- Review monthly to identify what worked

**When This Strategy Works Best**

**Ideal Scenarios**

✅ You're spending $50+ per day on Google Ads
✅ You have 50+ conversions per month total
✅ You have a clear new vs. repeat customer tracking system
✅ You've plateaued with PMax-only approach
✅ You have budget flexibility to restructure

**When to Wait**

⏸️ You're spending less than $50/day (stick with simple Shopping structure)
⏸️ You're just launching (build conversion history first)
⏸️ You don't have backend attribution setup yet

**The Bottom Line**

**How do you acquire more new customers?**

Not by pouring more money into Performance Max. By investing in the *first click* - the actual top-of-funnel moment when someone discovers your brand.

**The Math:**

- More cold traffic feeder campaigns = More new visitors
- More new visitors = Larger warm audience pool
- Larger warm audience pool = Better PMax performance
- Better PMax performance = Lower overall CAC

**Think of it this way:**

- Standard Shopping & Non-Brand Search = Discovery
- Performance Max = Conversion optimization
- Together = Sustainable growth

The companies "printing money" with Google Ads in 2026 aren't the ones blindly trusting Performance Max metrics. They're the ones who've taken back control of the customer journey, properly attributed their acquisition sources, and built campaign structures that work *with* Google's algorithms instead of against them.
