# Brand New or Don't Want Feeder Strategy

*Converted from `PPC Co Pilot/Performance Max for Ecommerce/Fundamentals/Brand New or Don't Want Feeder Strategy.rtfd`*

**Alternative Approaches**

Not every advertiser needs to implement the feeder strategy immediately - nor should they. Here are two other paths that can be taken:

**Option 1: Standard Shopping First Strategy**

![f335639990b84065badc17ac510dc675e921014aa7334e299d43d43733515143.png](Brand%20New%20or%20Don%27t%20Want%20Feeder%20Strategy.assets/f335639990b84065badc17ac510dc675e921014aa7334e299d43d43733515143.png)

**Why This Approach Works**

This is the safer, more data-driven path. By validating winners in Standard Shopping first, you give Performance Max proven products with conversion history, allowing the algorithm to optimize faster while you maintain more control over the learning phase.

**Best for:**

- New accounts (less than 3 months old)
- Smaller budgets (under $3,000/month)
- Testing product-market fit
- Risk-averse advertisers
- Those who want more control

**Step 1: Build Your Foundation with Standard Shopping**

**Start with Standard Shopping campaigns as your primary acquisition engine**

- Set up a well-structured Standard Shopping campaign targeting your best-selling products
- Use aggressive bidding to capture cold traffic
- Focus on non-brand search terms
- Exclude brand terms (save those for dedicated brand campaigns)

**Goal:** Achieve 30-50 conversions per month at your desired ROAS target

This conversion volume is critical because:

- It provides sufficient data for Google's smart bidding algorithms
- It validates which products actually convert at profitable rates
- It gives you confidence in your pricing and targeting before scaling

**Step 2: Identify Your Top Performers**

Once your Standard Shopping campaign is stable and hitting targets, analyze:

- **Best-selling products:** Which SKUs, Brands, or Product Types consistently convert?

**Don't rush this step.** Let your Standard Shopping campaigns run for at least 4-6 weeks to gather meaningful data.

**Step 3: Graduate Winners to Performance Max**

**Move your proven top performers into a Performance Max campaign:**

- Create a new PMax campaign featuring only your validated best sellers
- Structure Asset Groups around your top-performing product categories
- Set your tROAS target to match what these products achieved in Standard Shopping
- Keep your Standard Shopping campaign running for mid/low performers

**Why this works:**

- You're giving PMax products that already have proven conversion history
- The algorithm can optimize faster with winners
- You maintain control over what gets the most budget

**Step 4: Scale Performance Max**

**Once your PMax campaign is running smoothly:**

- Gradually increase budget in 10-20% increments
- Monitor ROAS closely - expect some dip as you scale into new audiences
- Give the campaign 1-2 weeks to stabilize after each budget increase
- Continue scaling until you hit a plateau

**Signs you've hit a plateau:**

- ROAS consistently declines despite optimization efforts
- Increasing budget doesn't yield proportional conversion growth
- New customer acquisition costs climb significantly
- You're spending more but getting the same number of sales

**Step 5: Consider the Feeder Strategy**

**When to implement the feeder approach:**

Once you've plateaued with your PMax campaign and can't scale further without sacrificing ROAS, it's time to test the feeder strategy:

- Build out Standard Shopping and non-brand Search as "feeder campaigns"
- Run these campaigns at aggressive bids to capture cold traffic
- Let PMax operate at a higher tROAS to act as the remarketing/conversion engine
- Exclude brand from PMax and feeders (run dedicated brand campaigns)
- Monitor how the feeder traffic flows through to PMax conversions

**Option 2: Direct to Performance Max Strategy**

**Why This Approach Works**

If you're confident in your product selection, have a solid budget, and want to move faster, you can start directly with Performance Max. This approach is more aggressive but can work well for established brands or when migrating from Smart Shopping.

**Best for:**

- Established brands with proven products
- Migrating from Smart Shopping campaigns
- Budgets of $3,000+/month
- Faster implementation timeline
- Experienced advertisers comfortable with automation

**Step 1: Launch Performance Max**

**Start with a well-structured Performance Max campaign**

- Launch with your best-selling products and proven categories
- Ensure you have at least $50/day budget ($1,500/month minimum)
- Set up proper conversion tracking and enhanced conversions
- Use optimized product feed
- Add quality assets (images, videos, ad copy)
- Include audience signals based on your best customer data

**Initial Setup Requirements:**

- Optimized product feed
- Enhanced conversion tracking
- Quality creative assets
- Proper brand exclusions
- Dedicated brand campaigns running separately

**Step 2: Build Data Foundation**

**Run the campaign until you achieve stable performance**

- Target: 30-50 conversions per month minimum
- This typically takes 4-8 weeks depending on budget
- Start with Max Conversion Value bidding (no tROAS cap initially)
- Let the algorithm learn your account patterns
- Monitor performance but avoid making major changes during learning phase

**What to Monitor:**

- Conversion volume trends
- Cost per conversion trends
- Product performance in insights
- Search term categories
- Audience performance

**Step 3: Optimize & Scale**

**Once you have stable conversion data:**

- Switch to tROAS bidding at 10-20% above current performance
- Gradually increase budget in 10-20% increments
- Allow 1-2 weeks between budget changes
- Optimize assets based on performance data
- Refine audience signals if needed
- Adjust product segmentation based on insights

**Scaling Guidelines:**

- Never increase budget more than 20% at once
- Smart bidding needs time to adjust to new budget levels
- Watch for ROAS decline as you scale
- Be patient - rushing scale often backfires

**Step 4: Hit Plateau**

**Eventually, every Performance Max campaign plateaus**

You'll know you've hit plateau when:

- Increasing budget no longer increases conversions proportionally
- ROAS declines despite optimization efforts
- New customer acquisition stalls
- Cost per acquisition rises significantly
- Backend data shows high repeat customer percentage

**This is normal and expected.** PMax naturally gravitates toward the easiest conversions (warm traffic, remarketing, brand searches, repeat customers).

**Step 5: Add Feeder Strategy**

**Time to regain control of cold traffic acquisition**

Implement the feeder strategy to break through the plateau:

- Launch Standard Shopping Non-Brand campaigns
- Launch Non-Brand Search campaigns
- Run these "feeder" campaigns with aggressive bidding (lower tROAS)
- Let PMax continue running at higher tROAS as remarketing engine
- Ensure brand is excluded from all non-brand campaigns
- Monitor how cold traffic from feeders converts through PMax

**The Strategy:**

- Feeders = Cold traffic acquisition (aggressive bidding)
- PMax = Remarketing & conversion engine (conservative bidding)
- Brand Campaigns = Protect brand traffic separately
